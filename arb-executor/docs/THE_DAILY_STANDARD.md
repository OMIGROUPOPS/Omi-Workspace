## LAYER 1 — THE THREE-BUCKET ENTRY GRADE (founding dispatch, operator's frame verbatim)
Every entry decision since midnight lands in exactly one of:
- **SETTLED** — did the thesis realize — **a W2-entered winner still grades F**.
- **OPEN** — is this position what doctrine ordered right now, exit cited.
- **POSTED-UNFILLED** — was not-filling correct — refusal counterfactuals priced, expired aims graded vs realized lows.

Window-stamped, per category, nightly, self-run.
**Runs as:** the THREE-BUCKET ENTRY GRADE section of the nightly adjudication (`analysis/conviction_replay.py`, 12:20 AM cron). Census key: `L1_three_bucket`.

## LAYER 2 — TWO-LAYER GRADING, IN THIS ORDER (founding dispatch, verbatim)
**A: the leg as an individual, first, always** — basis · discount bucket at fill (vs trailing traded mean) · required swing to band stated beside basis (**the favorites law, verbatim: higher swings in great contention, dropped where the path shows no promise**) · TIGHT/EARLY vs its own low · placement-phase and fill-phase · own verdict.
**B: the pair as a composition, second, never instead** — combined ≤97 as the measurement · orientation (riser early, faller late) · who-first vs fitted 2:1 · **neither layer launders the other.**
**Runs as:** Layer A + Layer B sections of every GAME REPORT (`analysis/game_report.py`). Census key: `L2_game_reports`.

## LAYER 3 — W1-VITALITY + THE CASH-WINDOW STAMP, AT ENTRY (founding dispatch, verbatim)
The fingerprint besides price — flow trajectory (DORMANT→WAKING→OPEN **transitions, not levels**) · realized volume's path to the floor (accretion vs burst) · category fill-regime (mains = join-regime, par-locked, timing-not-depth — receipts cited) · side timing anatomy (favorite dips first 2:1, dog's floor is its close, CHALL's soggy 160-min bottom vs ITF's 3) · prints-under-the-wall vs quote theater · clock honesty per category.
**Plus CASH-WINDOW ESTIMATE stamped on every entry:** P(band prints in W1/corridor) from the cell's fitted travel — **GOLD-PATH or W2-DEPENDENT, known at purchase**, sized and graded against the right expectation (a 90s exit = W2-dependent by construction, stamped as such).
**Runs as:** the `cash_window` + `w1_vitality` surfaces on every entry dossier (live_v4 `_entry_dossier`). Census key: `L3_cash_window`.
**Implementation status (honest, census-tracked):** cash_window verdict LIVE (basis = the w1_cohort gun-axis `lawful_share`, the recut's fitted share of harvest landing pre-gun; GOLD-PATH at ≥0.5, DECREED threshold, cited) · flow trajectory TRANSITIONS = the `flow_rest_seed` flip events (LIVE) · realized-volume path (accretion vs burst) = **GAP, fit owed** · side timing anatomy = the fitted pages cited via w1_cohort/dip_timing (LIVE as consultations) · prints-under-the-wall vs quote theater = **GAP, fit owed** · clock honesty = honest_clock surface (LIVE).

## THE GAME REPORT (the standard's artifact — founding dispatch Part 2)
Per game, generated by the OS daily and on-demand (`python3 analysis/game_report.py --date YYYYMMDD [--event KX...]`):
1. **Header** — legs, category, scheduled vs honest clock, discovery volume, floor status.
2. **The window timeline** established per game, every action plotted on it (W1 / CORRIDOR / W2 boundaries; placements, re-aims, gun, grace, cancels, fills, exits, settles).
3. **Layer A per leg** (the individual, first).
4. **Layer B per pair** (the composition, second, never instead).
5. **The decision log** — every placement/re-aim/refusal/cancel with surfaces consulted and their answers.
6. **Refusal counterfactuals** — what the refused/expired aims would have met on the tape.
7. **Verdict line** — grade, dollars, billed book (TAIL/PATH), owning fix.
Output: `.claude/game_reports/<date>/GR_<event>.md` + `INDEX.md`. Nightly: invoked by the adjudication run; on-demand: any seat, any time.

## THE NUMBER PAIRING LAW (C-PROVE-THE-DAY Part 5 amendment, operator word 07-15, PERMANENT)

## THE PARTICIPATION LABEL LAW (C-THREE-DAY-SOLIDIFY Part 4, operator word 07-15, PERMANENT)
Two participations exist and are never conflated: **P-offered** (the executor grade: taken / lawful-offered — did the doctrine take what its own lawful window offered) and **P-market** (the funnel grade: taken / every listed market — how much of the world's slate the fund touches). **No participation figure prints anywhere without its label.** An unlabeled participation is a named defect on the surface that printed it.

## THE LEG COLUMNS LAW (C-THREE-DAY-SOLIDIFY Part 3, PERMANENT)
No leg is reported anywhere — DAY SHEET, GAME REPORTS, nightly — without: fill window + placement window · cost (basis, size, role, band, discount bucket) · **exit required (band ¢ AND % of basis)** · highest tick toward exit after fill (its window + shortfall if the band went unmet) · worst tick · close (exit/flatten/settle, realized $, both grades). Columns whose input is the full exchange tape (highest/worst tick) are GAP-named on live surfaces until the tape column lands nightly; the three-day LEGS artifact is their founding population.

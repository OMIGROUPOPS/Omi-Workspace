# Execution and interpretation notes

- V1 outputs, its script and its frozen contract were preserved. This challenge
  has separate code, reports and durable per-question checkpoints.
- Eight workers were retained after inspecting RAM: 16,547,040 KiB visible,
  3,195,092 KiB free while the CHALL workers were active. No worker-count or
  scientific-definition change was made in response to the profile.
- Vectorized validation floor/timing/CRPS losses matched scalar reference
  calculations in 120 synthetic comparisons and all 274 callable experts on
  the sampled real MAIN receipt KXATPMATCH-26MAY01BLOZVE at 480 minutes.
- Evaluation omits constructing path distance only when no selected expert
  reads it. The serial verifier still constructs the path and checks every
  stored forecast on five filed-quantile-selected queries per tour.
- When an older-admitting diagnostic has no earlier comparable validation,
  it explicitly retains the same exact tick control, rather than fabricating
  April abstentions. Its stored expert names and older-member counts expose
  that fallback. It is not an April test of an older-trained key.
- Raw model curves include missed_reference_calls. Rows with a positive value
  were disqualified before selection: their partial-support accumulators are
  not comparable validation scores, including zero accumulators on no calls.
  Only complete-support scores are used in the published validation table.
- The exact tick control here means the tick-source cohort at the filed
  minute conversion B. It is not asserted to recreate native per-second A
  bit-for-bit. Both the date-only boundary and A are separately audited.
- Equal-game means, matched receipt losses and all-opportunity coverage have
  different denominators. Neither historical re-testing nor the sampled
  serial/parallel check is described as fresh confirmation or two full passes.

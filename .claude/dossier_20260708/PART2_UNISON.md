## §0 — Conventions (read before the table)

- **Cohort:** 174 pairs (348 legs) with ≥1 buy fill in the window under the 23:12 ET Jul-7 boot. 293 legs filled: **70 clean (pre-crash) · 213 M (post-02:52 crash — bot dead, stale resting bids filling mechanically, naked-class) · 10 MR (post-11:30 restart adoption wave)**.
- **Evidence source = exchange truth via Kalshi public API** (trades + 1-min candlesticks, pulled fresh for all 348 legs). This was forced and is also stronger: the VPS collectors died with the disk (tick/trade CSVs hole from ~02:46 to ~11:30 ET — exactly where most fills landed). Public prints have full coverage of the hole. VPS read-only throughout.
- **W1 window per pair = [23:12 ET boot, honest start), capped 11:45 ET.** Honest start = bot latch where logged (rare — bot dead), else **tape-inferred onset** (first sustained-activity burst: ≥40 trades or ≥150 shares in 10 min AND ≥100 trades or ≥400 shares in 30 min, both legs combined, plus ≥5c displacement — the same volume-burst family the bot's own latch uses). 171/174 starts are tape-inferred (`tape~`), 3 pairs never started by 11:45.
- **Conservative fill convention (strict):** a counterfactual fill at price P is claimed ONLY where actual **sell-flow prints (taker_side=no)** occurred at or below P inside W1 while P was expressible. Quote-touch without prints = NO CLAIM. Prints strictly below P = level swept (strong); prints at-P-only with <5 shares are flagged **queue-risk**.
- **Divot-conditioning gate (POST_FILL_MOVE classifier, minute grain):** a dip is claimable only if at its print the pre-print ask held within 2c over the next 30 min AND the bid recovered to ≥P (DIVOT), or the level was never undercut with ask holding (NO_UNDERCUT). Reprice-throughs (ask follows down ≥3c, mid stays below) are NOT claimable — deepening into them buys the no-bueno class (65–81% band-unreachable).
- **Expression law:** claim P requires the non-self chain to have shown bid ≥ P−1 at some point after boot and before the claim print (join or improve-1; below-chain rests legal at ≤ chain). Checked mechanically on candle bid-highs; passes trivially for true dips.
- **Walk caps (ITF_W 20 / ITF_M 14 / CHALL 2 / MAIN 1):** the unison claim is a static rest at the leg's own dip — no walk journey is required, so the cap never binds a claim; it binds only the real engine's chase. Claims sitting >12c under the prevailing ask are flagged **beyond built-aim depth** (the built aim tables/AIM_V2 would not conceive that deep; those claims lean on early below-chain resting).
- **Band clamp [5,95):** claims clipped to ≥5c (C-BAND-CLAMP is now enforcement); sub-5c prints noted where they existed.
- **Combined is used here as the task's descriptive scoreboard** (actual vs achievable vs S-line). Per the 07-07 ruling S is graded per-leg in the ledger; the S-line columns below are descriptive, not a grading claim.

## §1 — Headline findings

1. **The canvas was silent, not refused-at-price: 224/348 legs (64%) printed ZERO W1 sell-flow.** FLOOR-BY-HOUR reproduced live: the overnight ITF/CHALL book is a wide quote lattice; prints only arrive near the start burst. Under the strict prints-only convention there is nothing to claim on most legs.
2. **227 of 293 filled legs (77%) filled AFTER the tape-inferred start — last night's book was mostly a dead bot's stale bids acting as in-play liquidity** (213 legs are post-crash mechanical). The slate is NOT an entry-quality read; it is a naked-class exhibit (consistent with the 07-08 vault entry).
3. **Where the unison counterfactual exists at all (28/174 pairs both-claimable), it buys almost nothing: median delta 0c (ITF_W +1c).** Fills that did land in W1 landed at or within ~1-2c of their own divot-qualified claimable lows — the leak is not "we paid above the dip", it is "the pair never had two claimable dips under this clock".
4. **Unison ≤97 was claimable on only 14/174 pairs; unison ≤ cat S-line on exactly 1 (JONJEA 46+44=90, WTA_CHALL).** 14 both-claimable pairs had achievable >97 — the canvas refused ≤97 even in the counterfactual. Meanwhile 80/119 both-filled pairs ACTUALLY combined ≤97 — largely via in-play mechanical fills on collapsing legs, which is the naked-class shape, not the doctrine shape.
5. **The named tension: the S-line lives in the zone the built logic refuses to trade.** PAIR_STORY's floors (ITF 84, med bestT −15/−17 min before the bell) sit inside the final pre-bell burst window. This replay ends W1 at the first sustained burst — which is what the bot's own tape-latch cancel authority does. Under the machine's own clock, the floor's habitat is amputated: claims at the burst's leading edge are exactly what the latch cancels away. The unison counterfactual under the BUILT logic therefore cannot reach the S-lines the corpus says the canvas "typically offers" — the two use different clocks. This is a doctrine-level finding, not a replay artifact: either the latch is premature (median +22 min per the match-start memo) and is spending the floor to buy liveness safety, or the S-line convention over-credits pre-bell prints the engine may never touch. Both cannot be true at once. → BOARD-worthy.
6. **Unconditioned floors barely differ from conditioned ones** (both-filled pairs where both legs printed: uncond median ITF_W 95 vs conditioned 96; ATP_CHALL 99/98) — the divot-conditioning gate is NOT what blocks the counterfactual; print scarcity is.

## §4 — Sim-flattery flags (every place this replay could flatter, and what was done)

- **F1 · Start-time inference (systemic, 171/174 pairs):** no real bells exist for this slate (observed_starts is tour-only; the bot was dead for most latches). Tape-inferred onset is premature-biased (volume-burst family: median +22 min early per the match-start memo). Premature starts SHRINK W1 → this replay UNDER-claims (starves, not flatters) — except for matches already in-play at boot with quiet tapes (26JUL07/06 carryovers, flagged per-pair), where a late-detected onset could let in-play prints masquerade as W1. 18 pairs carry the carryover flag.
- **F2 · Queue priority:** a print AT the claim price does not guarantee a resting bid there fills (queue ahead). Claims with at-price-only evidence and <5 shares are flagged per-pair (**queue-risk**, 24 claims). Claims with prints strictly below, or ≥5 shares, are treated as sweeps. Historical queue depth is unknowable — this is the residual flattery in every claimed price.
- **F3 · Minute-grain divot conditioning:** the ask-hold test runs on 1-min candles (the VPS tick tape has the crash hole), vs the tick-grain POST_FILL classifier. Sub-minute reprice-throughs can pass as DIVOT. Strictness elsewhere (EDGE discard, sell-flow-only prints) leans against this.
- **F4 · Self-evidence:** the public tape includes the bot's own fills; some claims are evidenced by the very fill being counterfactualed (e.g. claims that equal the fill price at the fill minute). This is exchange truth — a bid there DID fill — but it means "claimable" sometimes restates "what happened" rather than beating it. Deltas of 0 are exactly this.
- **F5 · Capacity/liveness assumption:** the counterfactual assumes a live bot resting at both dips all night. The real bot was dead 02:52–11:30; a LIVE bot would have latched and cancelled many of the resting bids that actually filled in-play. The actual ≤97 rate (80/119) is therefore NOT reproducible by the fixed bot — do not read "actual beat counterfactual" as the naked-class being good; it is uncompensated risk that happened to print cheap entries.
- **F6 · EDGE discards:** claims within 5 min of W1 end were refused (structure unverifiable). 14 legs' lowest evidence died on this rule — conservative, named.
- **F7 · Sibling resting state** is from the 12:01 ET post-restart orders snapshot (boot reconcile had already reposted) — it describes NOW, not the overnight resting price.

## §6 — Verdict line

Under the built logic's own rules — honest latch clock, prints-only, divot-conditioned, expression-legal, band-clamped — **the unison counterfactual was available on 28 of 174 pairs, would have improved the achieved combined by a median of ~0-1c, reached ≤97 on 14 pairs, and reached the cat S-line on exactly one pair.** The canvas did not offer last night's slate at the S-lines under this clock; what it did offer, the (dead) machine mostly already took at in-play prices the doctrine cannot claim credit for.

# V24 landing estimator and phased arming

V24 is one replay variant of otherwise unchanged V23. It fits q25/q50/q75 own-close landing deltas on causal walk-forward training rows, excludes the five exact-start games, abstains on the 339 unresolved mirror identities, uses q50 as the central signing estimate with q25/q75 retained as uncertainty bounds, holds the mirror until its own coherent decline ordinal releases it, and retains the pair cap. No elapsed time enters a pair decision.

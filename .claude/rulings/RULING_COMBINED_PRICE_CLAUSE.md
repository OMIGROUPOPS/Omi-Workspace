# OPERATOR RULING — THE COMBINED-PRICE CLAUSE (2026-07-08 night, verbatim-as-relayed; supersedes every prior 97-framing)

THE PRINCIPLE: no fixed number defines a good entry. The combined price of a pair is a MEASUREMENT with exactly one honest reference — what THIS pair's tape actually offered — and that reference is computed from the historics per cell, not decreed.

THE LOGIC, derived (lineage cited, all on the honest axis):
1. Each leg's achievable discount is a FITTED quantity: edge_p50(cat, cell) = the median fillable dip below that leg's own W1-close, measured across the corpus per (category × 1¢ price cell), prints-only conservative convention (SEQFLOOR_RECUT 07-08, 4,870 legs → 538 populated cell rows; recut_cells.json).
2. The structure is cell-conditional, not fav/dog, not flat: edge ≈ 0 below the ~50-55 crossover (ITF; ~80 CHALL) — those legs' floors ARE their closes, arriving in the final minutes; edge rises smoothly with price to 6-19¢ median (p75 19-27¢) in the 75-94 cells — the heavy legs, where the bleed and the opportunity coincide (the crater class's own homes).
3. Therefore the pair's DYNAMIC FLOOR = Σ over both legs of (own W1-close − edge_p50(cat, cell)) — each leg hunted at ITS OWN divot at ITS OWN time (the seesaw guarantees the moments differ; inter-divot gap p50 41-62 min). This number is DIFFERENT FOR EVERY PAIR: a 30/70 Challenger pair floors near 92; an 8/92 ITF pair floors near 79. One fixed line cannot grade both.
4. Success = each leg within N¢ of its own fillable low (gold census N=4), independently priced (provenance clause), at the right time (divot, not reprice — ask-hold conditioning). The combined then lands at the floor as a CONSEQUENCE. Grade the decision per leg; read the combined as the receipt.

THE ROLES, fixed:
- 97 = the goal-law BOUND (interim completion arithmetic — dies at the cell-aim ramp; its census re-prints nightly as DRIFT-3, a countdown, never an achievement) and the emergency ceiling thereafter. Nothing else.
- 100 = par; >100 = total failure regardless of fills (unchanged, operator verbatim).
- Progress = the gap between achieved combined and the pair's dynamic floor, and per-leg dip-catch rate (the POSITION_GRADE §3 table).

CONTAMINATION RULE: any pass/fail at a fixed combined grades a 96 and an 85 identically while the fitted floors say most 96s underpaid the tape — therefore NO report, renderer, or agent may headline a "≤97 rate" (or any fixed-line rate) without the dynamic-floor gap printed beside it. The 94%-at-exactly-97 census line is the interim's signature, read only as its remaining lifespan.
